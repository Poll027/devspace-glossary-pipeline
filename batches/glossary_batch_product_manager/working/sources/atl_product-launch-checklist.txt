Product launch checklist: How to ensure a successful launch
By Atlassian
Get the free product launch template
Introduce your product to the market by starting with a clear and strategic launch plan.
Use template
Key Takeaways
A product launch checklist outlines key steps for introducing a new product, from market research to post-launch evaluation.
It ensures thorough preparation, coordinated execution, and alignment across teams.
Best practices include early planning, clear goals, multi-channel promotion, and feedback collection.
Use a launch checklist to organize activities, track progress, and maximize your product’s market impact.
Launching a new product is exciting for any company but can also be daunting. There are many moving parts to consider, from product development and testing to branding and marketing. A successful product launch requires careful planning and execution to ensure your new product release resonates with your target audience.
This guide reviews the key steps of a product launch checklist and highlights best practices and tools such as Confluence. These help streamline the process and serve as valuable product management tools. The article also answers questions about product launches to help you confidently prepare for your launch day.
What is a product launch?
A product launch is the process of introducing a new product to the market. It involves coordinated activities to create excitement, attract customers, and drive sales. A product launch can be for a new product or an update to an existing product line.
Importance of a successful product launch
A successful product launch helps establish your product’s market position and differentiate it from competitors. A well-executed launch can generate buzz and excitement, which attracts early adopters and influencers who can help spread the word about your new product release.
A successful launch can also boost your brand’s reputation and credibility. Teams accomplish this by delivering high-quality items and services with compelling product features that meet or exceed customer expectations. This can lead to positive reviews, referrals, and repeat business.
A successful product launch can significantly affect a team’s revenue and growth. A strong launch can drive initial sales and set the stage for ongoing demand. It can also attract investors and partners who can help fuel future growth and expansion.

Key steps of a product launch checklist
A product launch checklist outlines all the tasks and activities necessary to bring a new product release to market. The checklist should align with and support the overall product development strategy. By following a product launch template, teams can ensure that they take all critical steps and that everyone is working toward the same goals. 
Here are some key steps for a new product launch checklist.
Pre-launch preparation
Before you can launch a product, there's a lot of groundwork:
Conduct market research to validate your product idea and identify the target audience.
Develop product roadmaps and set launch goals and objectives.
Create a brand identity and messaging framework.
Build your go-to-market strategy, including pricing, packaging, and distribution channels.
The product manager is responsible for defining the go-to-market strategy and aligning all the cross-functional teams involved in the launch.
Check out these tips for presenting product roadmaps.

Pages offer flexible documentation, including text, images, code, tables, etc.
Templates provide quick-start frameworks for project overviews, plans, milestones, tracking, and beyond.
Spaces organize project documents into one area to keep related information together and make permissions a breeze.
Product development and testing
This phase includes several critical steps:
Design and develop your product or service.
Conduct user beta testing and gather feedback.
Evaluate data from product analytics to optimize the user experience.
Prepare for launch by finalizing packaging, documentation, and support channels.
Throughout the development process, Confluence helps your team:
Collaborate on product requirements and specifications.
Provide feedback and approval on designs and prototypes.
Track progress and identify blockers or dependencies.
Document test results and user feedback.
Branding and marketing
Generating buzz and anticipation around your product launch is critical to driving initial sales and adoption. It helps teams:
Develop a compelling brand story and messaging that resonates with your target audience.
Design creative assets such as logos, websites, and marketing collateral.
Plan and execute marketing campaigns across email, social media, PR, and advertising channels.
Prepare sales teams and channel partners to sell and support the new product.
Confluence can be a central hub for all your branding and marketing activities. It can help you:
Brainstorm and refine messaging and product positioning.
Collaborate on creative briefs and design assets.
Plan and track marketing campaigns and deliverables.
Distribute launch materials to sales teams and partners.
Sales and distribution
A solid sales and distribution strategy helps get your product into customers’ hands. You can:
Identify and onboard channel partners and resellers.
Create sales collateral such as pitch decks, demos, and battle cards.
Set up e-commerce and fulfillment systems.
Train support and sales teams on the new product.
Streamline sales and distribution with Confluence, which can help you:
Create and share sales playbooks and training materials.
Collaborate on partner agreements and contracts.
Track sales pipelines and forecasts.
Monitor customer feedback and support requests.
Logistics and operations
Be sure to organize logistical and operational activities This helps teams:
Manage inventory and the supply chain.
Coordinate shipping and delivery.
Set up customer success channels and processes.
Prepare for post-launch activities such as returns and exchanges.
Use Confluence to help keep all these moving parts organized and on track. With Confluence, you can:
Document processes and procedures.
Assign and track tasks and deliverables.
Integrate with tools like Jira for bug tracking and project management.
Provide a central repository for all launch-related information.
Launch day
It’s all hands on deck to ensure a smooth rollout when launch day finally arrives. Be sure to:
Monitor systems and address any technical issues that arise.
Execute marketing campaigns and track performance.
Engage with early customers and gather feedback.
Celebrate the team’s hard work and success.
On launch day, Confluence can serve as a central command center to:
Track real-time metrics and key performance indicators.
Triage and resolve issues as they come up.
Communicate status updates and key decisions.
Capture lessons learned and areas for improvement.
Post-launch evaluation
The post-launch period is critical for gathering feedback, iterating on your product, and setting the stage for future growth. Make sure you:
Analyze sales and marketing data to assess launch performance.
Gather customer feedback through surveys, interviews, and usage data.
Identify areas for improvement and make necessary updates.
Plan for future releases and enhancements.
Confluence can help teams stay organized and aligned during the post-launch phase. Use it to:
Capture and synthesize customer feedback and insights.
Prioritize and plan future product iterations.
Conduct post-mortem meetings and document lessons learned.
Celebrate wins and recognize team contributions.
Product launch best practices
To set your product launch up for success, keep the following best practices in mind:
Start planning early and involve all key stakeholders from the beginning.
Set clear goals and metrics for success and track progress along the way.
Focus on solving customer pain points and validate your assumptions with research and testing.
Develop a compelling brand story and messaging that differentiates your product from competitors.
Execute a multi-channel marketing campaign that reaches your target audience where they are.
Prepare your support and sales teams to handle the influx of inquiries and orders.
Gather feedback and iterate based on customer insights and data.
By following these best practices and staying organized and aligned using tools such as Confluence, you can increase your chances of a successful product launch that drives growth and customer satisfaction.

Product launch checklist: Frequently asked questions
What is key to a successful product launch?
Several factors contribute to a successful product launch:
Plan and prepare thoroughly. Involve all key stakeholders.
Understand your target audience, their needs, and pain points.
Create a compelling brand story and messaging that differentiates your product.
Define and execute a multi-channel marketing campaign that generates buzz and demand.
Prepare and train a sales and support team to handle inquiries and orders.
Focus on gathering customer feedback and iterate based on insights and data.
How far in advance should you start preparing for a product launch?
The ideal timeframe for initiating pre-launch preparations depends on several factors, including product complexity, team size, and market dynamics. Start planning at least 6-12 months before your target launch date. This allows enough time to conduct thorough market research, develop and test your product, create marketing and sales materials, and coordinate logistics and operations.
How do you know if your product is ready for launch?
There are several key criteria and checkpoints you can use to assess the readiness of your product for launch. These include product quality, market fit, competitive differentiation, sales and marketing readiness, and operational preparedness.

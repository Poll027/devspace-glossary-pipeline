Capture ideas and insights
Gather your product opportunities, feedback, and requests in one place and keep your discovery work structured from the start.
Prioritize with flexible frameworks
Compare ideas using custom fields, scoring, and the prioritization method that fits your team, so every decision is grounded in clear criteria.
Rally teams around what’s next
Create and share roadmaps tailored to any stakeholder - even ones outside your organization - and gather feedback through comments, votes, and reactions.
Connect discovery to delivery in Jira
Link product decisions to Jira execution so developers see the full context, roadmaps stay in sync, and teams close the loop when work ships.

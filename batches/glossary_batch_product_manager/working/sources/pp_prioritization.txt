Frequently Asked Questions
What is product prioritization, and why does it matter? 
Product prioritization is deciding which initiatives to focus on first, and why. Done well, every choice is strategy-linked and traceable to a customer signal. ProductPlan gives teams the structure to evaluate initiatives, surface evidence, and attach reasoning to every decision so the whole organization understands what's being built and why.
How does ProductPlan help teams make prioritization decisions with confidence?
ProductPlan gives every decision a structure: strategic fit and customer signal evidence. Traceable reasoning is attached to every initiative, so when stakeholders ask why something is on the roadmap, you have a clear, evidence-backed answer ready.
How does ProductPlan's Product Intelligence Platform help with prioritization?
It surfaces customer feedback and strategic context to help you evaluate which ideas are most likely to drive outcomes, with traceable evidence behind every suggestion.
How do I connect roadmap priorities to strategy? 
ProductPlan's Strategy module lets you define objectives before you prioritize. Every initiative links to the goal it supports, and Custom Views let you filter by objective for clean, executive-ready views without a separate slide deck.
How is ProductPlan different from Aha! for prioritization?
Aha! is a broad product lifecycle suite that requires significant configuration. ProductPlan is purpose-built for connecting customer signals to prioritized initiatives to a defensible roadmap. Teams get to their first strategy-linked roadmap in days. 
Can I show stakeholders why something was prioritized or deprioritized? 
Yes. Every initiative carries traceable reasoning: evidence, customer signals, and decision notes. When leadership asks about any roadmap decision, you have a complete, shareable answer in seconds.
What prioritization tools does ProductPlan support? 
ProductPlan includes a custom cost/benefit calculator so teams can evaluate initiatives in a way that reflects their own priorities. Customer signals and strategic context layer in to build a complete picture of what to build and why.
How do I align leadership when priorities need to shift? 
ProductPlan gives you a structured, evidence-backed conversation. You can show the evidence and customer signals behind the current plan and model the trade-off of any change. 60% of PMs say leadership escalations are the #1 reason priorities change (State of Product Management 2026).
Does ProductPlan integrate with Jira? 
Yes. ProductPlan has a native two-way sync with Jira Cloud, Server, and Data Center. Engineering teams work in Jira while ProductPlan serves as the strategic layer, connecting sprint-level work to prioritized initiatives and customer signals. ProductPlan also integrates with Azure DevOps.
How do I measure whether my prioritized initiatives are delivering results? 
Every initiative connects to a strategic objective, so progress is visible from the moment it hits the roadmap. As work moves through development, the original reasoning stays attached. Teams use this for more grounded quarterly reviews and sharper prioritization over time.

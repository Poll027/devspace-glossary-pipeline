Frequently Asked Questions
What types of roadmaps can I build in ProductPlan?
ProductPlan supports five views: Timeline, List, Table, Custom Views, and Portfolio View. Every roadmap is structured using Lanes, Legend, Tags, and Custom Dropdown Fields. Custom Views let you save separate versions for executives, engineering, or customers, all from the same live data source. Switching between layouts takes one click.
How do I share my roadmap with stakeholders who don’t have ProductPlan accounts?
Use Private Link to share a live, view-only roadmap with anyone. No ProductPlan account required. Generate a separate link per Custom View for different audiences. You can also embed your roadmap in Confluence, Jira, MS Teams, SharePoint, or Google Sites. Anyone you invite without an account gets a free View Only account automatically.
How does ProductPlan stay in sync with Jira?
ProductPlan has a native two-way sync with Jira (Cloud, Server, and Data Center) and Azure DevOps. Jira epics become Containers and user stories become roadmap Bars automatically. Dates, Lanes, and Legends stay current automatically or on demand. Push date changes from ProductPlan back to Jira in a single click.
How is ProductPlan different from Aha! for roadmapping?
Aha! is a broad product lifecycle suite that requires significant configuration. ProductPlan is purpose-built for roadmapping. Teams get to a shareable roadmap in days, not months. ProductPlan offers unlimited free viewer accounts, native embedding in Confluence and Jira, and Private Links that require no login.
How is ProductPlan different from Productboard?
Productboard is primarily a product discovery and prioritization tool. ProductPlan is built around the roadmap itself: sharing it, aligning stakeholders, and keeping it in sync with engineering. ProductPlan offers live roadmap embedding, unlimited free viewer accounts, and Private Links. Productboard requires a login for stakeholder sharing.
Can I manage multiple product roadmaps in one place?
Yes. Portfolio View (Professional and Enterprise) consolidates multiple roadmaps into a single filterable executive view. Filter by team, OKR, or time horizon. Roadmap Folders keep related roadmaps grouped and shared. Enterprise plans add centralized Tag Management and Shared Legends for consistency across every roadmap in the organization.
How do I connect roadmap items to OKRs and business outcomes?
ProductPlan's Strategy module lets you define OKRs before building your roadmap, so every initiative links directly to a strategic objective. Use the Prioritization Board to rank initiatives against those objectives before they hit the roadmap. Custom Views can be filtered by objective, so an executive-facing view shows only the initiatives tied to the goals they own. Set up OKRs first and the connection is structural, not a tag added after the fact.
How long does it take to build a roadmap in ProductPlan?
Most teams have their first shareable roadmap within a day or two of signing up. There is no implementation, no certification, and no dedicated admin required. Import existing data via CSV or drag items from the Parking Lot directly onto the roadmap. Lane and Legend data carries over automatically.
Does ProductPlan offer a free trial?
ProductPlan offers a self-guided product tour so you can experience the full platform before speaking to sales. To explore pricing plans and trial options, visit the ProductPlan pricing page or book a demo with the team.

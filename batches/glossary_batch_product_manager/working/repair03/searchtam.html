Frequently Asked Questions

ProductPlan is product management software that combines strategic roadmapping with AI-powered customer and market intelligence. As a product planning software platform, it gives product teams one connected place to capture customer insights, prioritize what to build, communicate the plan to every stakeholder, and manage portfolio visibility from discovery through launch.

Most product roadmap tools are built primarily for engineering organizations. ProductPlan is built for the entire product team and the stakeholders around it. It’s visual, accessible to non-technical stakeholders, right-sized for teams of 3 to 20+ PMs, and includes built-in Product Intelligence — AI-moderated research surveys connected to roadmap priorities in one platform.

Product Intelligence is ProductPlan’s AI-powered research platform. It lets product teams run AI-moderated surveys with their own customers or a verified research panel, synthesize responses in one click, and surface insights to inform roadmap priorities. The AI Moderator asks adaptive follow-up questions in real time, and the Research Agent can answer any question about your data in plain language.

The alerting signal can be a metric, a service level objective, or a recurring log signal turned into a metric ( OPS02-BP04 Regularly analyze workload logs ). Alert fatigue is the dominant failure mode, where operators receive hundreds of notifications a day and learn to ignore them. Then, the one alert that signals a real incident is lost with the rest. The usual cause is alerting on raw metrics. Instead, alert on conditions that represent actual problems. A single failing dependency can trip a dozen metri ions . Keep static thresholds for limits that do not move, and revisit both as the workload changes. Reduce noise with composite alarms: Group related alarms into composite alarms in Amazon CloudWatch so that a single root cause produces one meaningful alert rather than many. Base each composite condition on the failure it represents, not on individual metrics.

Benefits of establishing this best practice: Recovery procedures are proven against real faults, not assumed to work. Hidden weaknesses surface in a controlled setting where you can address them safely. Alerting and notification paths are validated as part of each experiment. Resilience improves continually as experiments become permanent regression tests.

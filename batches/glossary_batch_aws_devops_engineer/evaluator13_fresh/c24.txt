tb1: A release requirement allows only limited exposure to a new version and needs a way to return traffic to a known-good version. Compare delivery strategies by their replacement and traffic behavior, not by assuming one is always safest.
tb2: Rolling delivery replaces portions of the workload progressively rather than replacing all instances simultaneously.
tb3: Blue/green delivery maintains separate old and new environments and switches traffic to the new environment.

Canary delivery exposes a new version to a limited share of traffic before broader rollout.

Publish Approval Notifications You can configure an approval action to publish a message to an Amazon Simple Notification Service topic when the pipeline stops at the action. Amazon SNS delivers the message to every endpoint subscribed to the topic. You must use a topic created in the same AWS Region as the pipeline that will include the approval action. When you create a topic, we recommend you give it a name that will identify its purpose, in for proval action, you can also add comments that are displayed to those who receive the notifications or those who view the action in the console or CLI response. No Configuration Options You can also choose not to configure any of these three options. You might not need them if, for example, you can notify someone directly that the action is ready for their review, or you simply want the pipeline to stop until you decide to approve the action yourself.

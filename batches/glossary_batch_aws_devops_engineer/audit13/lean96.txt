
### 96 aws_devops.rightsize_a_resource_using_a_supplied_workload_baseline
INLINE supplied utilization baseline=supplied; rightsizing constraint=A capacity reduction is acceptable only when the supplied workload performance and availability evidence remains satisfied.; supplied workload baseline=supplied
f1 Rightsizing matches resource type and size to workload performance and capacity requirements rather than simply choosing the smallest instance.
f2 Compare supplied load, utilization and performance evidence with service constraints and recommend a smaller instance only when those constraints remain satisfied.
f3 Account for peak and changing demand in the baseline rather than using one low-utilization sample as sufficient evidence.
tb1 A workload appears lightly utilized, and the exercise asks whether a smaller instance is justified. Rightsizing must preserve performance and capacity requirements, including peak and changing demand.
tb4 Compare the supplied load, utilization and performance baseline with service constraints. Suppose one low-utilization sample suggests spare capacity, but the supplied peak period reaches the required performance boundary. Do not use the low sample alone to justify a smaller instance. A recommendation needs evidence that the proposed size still satisfies the supplied constraints across the relevant baseline. Record the workload requirement, observed baseline and reason to recommend or withhold a reduction. The objective is matching resource type and size to workload needs, not selecting the smallest available instance or inventing a price comparison. If the supplied baseline supports spare capacity even at relevant peaks, explain how the proposed size still satisfies the stated performance constraint. If evidence is insufficient, withhold the specific reduction and state what the current baseline establishes. That is more defensible than recommending a smaller size from a single low sample. Keep observed load, utilization and performance distinct from the recommendation they support.
tb5 Low utilization at one instant is not sufficient rightsizing evidence. Account for peak and changing demand and retain service constraints in the recommendation. Smaller is appropriate only when the supplied performance and capacity requirements remain satisfied.
assessment choose_smaller_ec2_capacity_only_when_service_constraints_are_preserved
assessment evaluate_peak_and_changing_demand_before_recommending_a_reduction
practice_anchor.type field_selection
practice_anchor.objective Select one EC2 sizing recommendation from supplied options using workload load, utilization, performance evidence and service constraints across normal, peak and changing demand. Select a smaller option only when the supplied evidence supports it; otherwise withhold a reduction.
practice_anchor.success The selected recommendation preserves every supplied performance and capacity constraint across the relevant baseline. Supporting evidence identifies the workload requirement and the normal, peak and changing-demand observations that justify the choice, rather than relying on one low-utilization sample.
visual_anchor.type data_table
visual_anchor.purpose Make supplied normal, peak and changing-demand load, utilization and performance evidence visible alongside service constraints and evidence for each candidate EC2 size, so the basis for recommending or withholding a reduction can be checked.
note Use supplied baseline and constraints; do not introduce EC2 pricing estimates or unsupported instance recommendations.

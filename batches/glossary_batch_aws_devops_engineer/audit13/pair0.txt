tb4 Take a supplied VPC range of 10.20.0.0/16. Two candidate subnets are 10.20.1.0/24 and 10.20.2.0/24. They lie inside that VPC and do not intersect. A /24 contains 256 addresses; after the five AWS reservations, 251 remain usable. For 10.20.1.0/24, the reserved addresses are .0 through .3 and .255.

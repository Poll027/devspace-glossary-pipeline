f2 https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/get-started/overview-of-formulas-in-excel LINE 184: A relative cell reference in a formula, such as A1, is based on the relative position of the cell that contains the formula and the cell the reference refers to. If the position of the cell that contains the formula changes, the reference changes. If you copy or fill the formula across rows or down columns, the reference automatically adjusts.

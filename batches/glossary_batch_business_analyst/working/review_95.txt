Choose whether recipients can reshare or build on the underlying data and grant only the permissions the audience needs. Sharing and access depend on license and capacity; sharing generally requires Pro or PPU, and free recipients require eligible Premium or Fabric capacity.
tb4 Select Share for the report or dashboard and specify recipients or an appropriate sharing link. Choose whether recipients can reshare or build on the underlying data, granting only the permissions the audience needs. In this hypothetical case, readers need to consume the report, so do not enable broader permissions merely because the options are available.

Check licensing and capacity conditions. Sharing generally requires Pro or PPU, and free recipients require eligible Premium or Fabric capacity. These are qualified access conditions, not a promise that any recipient with a link can open every shared artifact.

The useful output is a published report shared to its intended audience with considered permissions and access prerequisites. Keep workspace selection, recipient or link selection, permission choices, and license/capacity checks distinct. A successful publish does not establish that each intended recipient satisfies the sharing conditions.
tb5 A link does not by itself override licensing or capacity requirements. Publishing and sharing also address different steps. Grant reshare or build permissions only when needed, and preserve “generally” in the license statement rather than turning it into an unconditional universal rule.

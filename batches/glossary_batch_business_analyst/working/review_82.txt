Consider Developers' capacity and past performance in selection. A stakeholder’s strong preference does not replace that consideration. The BA can help clarify requirements and questions, while the planning discussion remains collaborative rather than becoming a BA-issued delivery promise.

The useful contribution is transparent candidate work with enough detail for the team to reason about selection. If an item cannot yet be understood, record and resolve the uncertainty instead of silently assuming its scope. The example does not prescribe how many items belong in the iteration; that decision depends on the collaborative assessment and Developers' capacity and past performance, not on a universal quota for business analysis work.

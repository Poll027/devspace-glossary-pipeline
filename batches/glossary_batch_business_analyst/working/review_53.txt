tb4 For a physical join, drag the first table to the canvas, double-click it, and then drag the second table into the join canvas. In the join dialog, choose the join type and matching fields. If the question requires all customers, place and preserve the named side appropriately; inner keeps matches, left or right preserves its named side, and full outer preserves both.

If the workflow uses an extract, select Extract on the Data Source page and configure it before saving. An extract is a saved snapshot for analysis, not a claim that it always shows the newest source state.

The useful output is a connection and prepared data arrangement suited to the reporting question. Keep the physical-join canvas step explicit: dragging tables without explaining the double-click entry to that canvas leaves a beginner without the named workflow. The example ends at preparation, not at worksheet charts or dashboard placement.
tb5 An extract is a saved snapshot, so do not describe it as inherently live. Also preserve the distinction between connecting, creating a physical join in its canvas, and selecting an extract. Those are different preparation choices and actions.

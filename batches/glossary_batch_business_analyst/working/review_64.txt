FACT f1 The sample mean is computed as the sum of the observed values divided by the number of observations.
FACT f2 The median is the middle value when the data are ordered from smallest to largest; if there is an even number of observations, the median is the average of the two middle values.
FACT f3 The mode is the value that occurs most often in the data; there can be more than one mode.
FACT f4 The mean is more strongly affected by extreme observations (such as those in the tail of a skewed distribution) than the median, which is more robust to them; for this reason the median can better represent a typical value in a skewed dataset.
tb1 Imagine five observed dispatch-preparation times in minutes: 2, 3, 3, 4, and 18. The mean is the sum divided by the number of observations: 30 / 5 = 6 minutes. The ordered middle value is 3, so the median is 3 minutes. The most frequent value is also 3, so the mode is 3 minutes.
tb2 The sample mean is computed as the sum of the observed values divided by the number of observations.
tb3 The median is the middle value when the data are ordered from smallest to largest; if there is an even number of observations, the median is the average of the two middle values. The mode is the value that occurs most often in the data; there can be more than one mode. The mean is more strongly affected by extreme observations (such as those in the tail of a skewed distribution) than the median, which is more robust to them; for this reason the median can better represent a typical value in a skewed dataset.
tb4 The extreme 18-minute observation affects the mean more strongly than the median. In this skewed sample, the median can better represent a typical value than the mean. Do not hide the extreme observation; explain why the two center summaries differ.

For an even-sized example, 2, 3, 4, and 9 has median (3 + 4) / 2 = 3.5. For mode, 2, 2, 4, and 4 has two most frequent values, so both 2 and 4 are modes. These examples apply the definitions rather than assuming every dataset has a single mode or a median that is an observed value.

The practical BA task is to interpret a center measure in the context of the business data. A report saying “average preparation time is 6 minutes” should be clear that it uses the mean. The median answers a different center question and is more robust to extreme observations.
tb5 Mean, median, and mode are not interchangeable names for one number. Order values before finding the median, average the two middle values when the count is even, and allow multiple modes. An extreme value can pull the mean away from the median.

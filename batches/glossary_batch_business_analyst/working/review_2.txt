tb1 Imagine dispatch, sales, and customer-support representatives disagreeing about which delivery instructions must be available before dispatch. A workshop brings them together for goal-oriented activities and discussion. Define its outcome as a shared account of the information needed, with any unresolved questions recorded.
tb2 A workshop uses goal-oriented group activities and discussion to produce shared outcomes.
tb3 Build the agenda by defining goals, identifying questions needed to reach them, and choosing activities that answer those questions. Allocate time to each workshop segment and prepare the materials needed to capture discussion. Guide discussion toward the agenda goals and analyze and share the collected outputs after the workshop.
tb4 Build the agenda from that goal. Questions might include “What must dispatch know?” and “Which team supplies each piece of information?” Choose activities that address those questions, such as having participants describe an order and then discuss the required information together. In this hypothetical agenda, allocate ten minutes to the goal, twenty to the order exercise, twenty to discussion, and ten to consolidating outputs. Prepare a shared capture sheet before the session so contributed requirements and questions have a place to go.

During discussion, guide contributions back to the agenda’s questions. A lengthy discussion of vehicle purchasing does not answer the agreed information question, so return to the intended outcome. Afterward, analyze the captured material and share the resulting requirements and unresolved questions with participants. The workshop is not finished merely because its time has elapsed: the collected outputs still need analysis and sharing. The example timings and activity are choices for this situation, not a mandatory workshop format.
tb5 A workshop is not simply several people receiving the same presentation. Its activities and discussion are directed at shared outcomes. A detailed agenda helps only when its questions and activities actually support the agreed goals.

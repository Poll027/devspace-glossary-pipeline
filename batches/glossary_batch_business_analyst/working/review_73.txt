tb4 In this hypothetical walkthrough, review the instruction-display requirement and its acceptance conditions, then make the unresolved meaning of “current” visible. Record decisions in the shared requirements document instead of letting meeting discussion become the only source of agreement.

Close the agenda by asking designated approvers to approve the reviewed requirements or state outstanding conditions. Record the approval outcome, the approvers, and any unresolved conditions in the shared decision log. For example, the record might state that approval remains conditional on clarifying the instruction-update rule. That is different from recording unconditional approval.

The useful output is a visible agreement and decision record. A demonstration can support discussion, but the fact that a feature was shown does not itself record approval. Keep the reviewed scope and unresolved questions connected to the decision so later readers can understand what was actually agreed and what remains open.
tb5 Attendance or a successful demonstration is not an approval record. Ask designated approvers for approval or outstanding conditions, then record the outcome and approvers. Do not rewrite a conditional outcome as full agreement merely because the walkthrough ended.

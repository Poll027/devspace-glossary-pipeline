FACT f1 Acceptance criteria state the conditions used to determine whether a particular user story is satisfied.
FACT f2 Discuss what completing the story means, make the acceptance conditions testable, and document that shared understanding.
FACT f3 Refinement adds detail to make backlog items more precise and understandable.
tb1 Suppose a story says dispatchers want to see current instructions so they can prepare the correct order. Acceptance criteria state the conditions used to judge whether this particular story is satisfied. Discuss what completing it means instead of relying only on the story’s short wording.
tb2 Acceptance criteria state the conditions used to determine whether a particular user story is satisfied.
tb3 Discuss what completing the story means, make the acceptance conditions testable, and document that shared understanding. Refinement adds detail to make backlog items more precise and understandable.
tb4 For this hypothetical case, the stakeholders agree that selecting an existing order displays its current instructions, and selecting an unknown identifier displays a not-found result. Make those acceptance conditions testable and document the shared understanding. “The lookup is good” does not describe a clear completion condition.

During refinement, add detail that makes the backlog item more precise and understandable. If stakeholders disagree about what “current” means, clarify that point before leaving the criterion as if everyone interpreted it identically. The example conditions are agreed choices for this story, not a complete checklist for all instruction-access systems.

The useful output is story-specific completion understanding that can be checked. It should connect directly to the desired behavior rather than becoming a broad quality slogan. Keep the conditions in the shared record so later discussion is about the documented understanding, not a different person’s recollection of what was meant.
tb5 Acceptance criteria belong to a particular story. They are not a substitute for clarifying what that story means, and vague positive wording does not make them testable. Discuss and document the shared completion conditions instead of assuming the title is sufficient.

f3 https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/collaborate-share/service-share-dashboards LINE 104: When you share a report through an app, recipients get read access to both the report and its underlying semantic model. Without RLS or OLS defined on the semantic model, the report is shared with full access to all the data, even when tables, columns, or measures aren't shown in the report. If users have a direct link to any content in your app, they can access all the data, even if the table, column, or measure is visually hidden in the app navigation. When you create an app, you can also grant users build permissions on the semantic model, which enables them to build new solutions on top of the data.

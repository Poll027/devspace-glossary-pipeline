If you’re looking to learn something qualitative or behavioral, there’s likely a method better suited to your needs. f2 https://www.nngroup.com/articles/survey-best-practices/ LINE 74: When drafting a survey, many researchers slip into autopilot and start by asking a plethora of demographic questions. Ask yourself: do you need all that demographic information? Will you use it to answer your research questions? Even if you will use it, is there another way to capture it besides asking about it in a survey? For example, if you are surveying current customers, and they are providing their email addresses, could you look up their demographic information if needed?

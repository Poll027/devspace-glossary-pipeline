FACT f1 Requirements traceability maintains links from initial business needs through design, development, and testing to the work that fulfills the requirements.
FACT f2 Construct an RTM with a row for each requirement identifier and columns for its originating business need or source, related design artifact, and test-case identifiers; populate these with references to the actual artifacts rather than unlinked descriptions.
FACT f3 When a requirement changes, follow its links to identify affected work items that need updating.
FACT f4 Review the RTM for missing required links: a requirement without a source link is orphaned from its business justification, and a missing design or test link is a coverage gap when that artifact is required for the stage being reviewed.
tb1 Suppose requirement R-12 says a dispatcher must see current instructions for a selected order. Its originating business need is reducing clarification delays. A traceability matrix keeps that connection alongside links to the work that fulfills and checks the requirement.
tb2 Requirements traceability maintains links from initial business needs through design, development, and testing to the work that fulfills the requirements.
tb3 Construct an RTM with a row for each requirement identifier and columns for its originating business need or source, related design artifact, and test-case identifiers; populate these with references to the actual artifacts rather than unlinked descriptions. When a requirement changes, follow its links to identify affected work items that need updating. Review the RTM for missing required links: a requirement without a source link is orphaned from its business justification, and a missing design or test link is a coverage gap when that artifact is required for the stage being reviewed.
tb4 Create a row for R-12 with columns for the originating need or source, related design artifact, and test-case identifiers. In this hypothetical record, link the source to Need-3, the design to Design-7, and the tests to Test-21 and Test-22. These labels stand for actual referenced artifacts in the exercise. A cell saying “some testing” is not the same as a reference to the test artifacts.

Now suppose R-12 changes to include an amended-instruction indicator. Follow its links to identify related design, development, and testing work that may need updating. The matrix helps you find affected work; the requirement change should not remain isolated in its own paragraph.

Review link coverage for the stage being considered. No source link leaves the requirement orphaned from its business justification. A missing design or test link is a coverage gap when that artifact is required at that stage. This qualification matters: do not demand a later-stage artifact merely because its column exists. The practical result is connected requirements and stage-appropriate evidence, not just a table filled with descriptions.
tb5 A matrix is not traceability merely because it has columns. Its entries must reference the supporting artifacts. Also distinguish a missing required link from a link to an artifact not yet required at the reviewed stage; the latter qualification should not be discarded.

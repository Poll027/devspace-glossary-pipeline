tb4 Refine unclear or oversized items. For the instruction item, clarify the description and break it into smaller, more precise items if needed. For the vague reporting item, establish the intended detail before treating it as ready. Add detail such as description, order, and size, then keep the backlog ordered.

Items that can be completed within one iteration are ready for selection; refinement usually provides the transparency needed for that judgment. Do not label an oversized item ready merely because it appears near the top. Conversely, refinement is not just changing order: it also improves detail and precision.

The useful BA contribution is clearer items and an understandable ordered backlog. Keep clarification, splitting, detail, and ordering visible rather than treating the backlog as a static archive.
tb5 A backlog is not simply an unordered requirements list. Refinement also does more than re-rank items: it makes them smaller and more precise with relevant details. High placement alone does not establish that an item fits within an iteration.

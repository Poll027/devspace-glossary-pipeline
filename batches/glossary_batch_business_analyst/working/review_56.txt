FACT f1 A gap is a specific difference between the current situation and the desired state.
FACT f2 Compare the current-state description with the desired-state objectives and list the specific differences keeping the business from those goals.
tb1 Imagine the current-state column says dispatch preparation takes twelve minutes under the assessed conditions, while the desired-state column sets a five-minute target under the same conditions. The specific time difference is seven minutes. Another current note says staff consult two instruction sources, while the desired state calls for current instructions available in one place.
tb2 A gap is a specific difference between the current situation and the desired state.
tb3 Compare the current-state description with the desired-state objectives and list the specific differences keeping the business from those goals.
tb4 Compare each current description with its corresponding desired objective. List the specific differences keeping the business from the goal: the preparation-time difference and the information-access difference in this hypothetical case. These are more precise than writing only “there is a dispatch gap.”

Keep the comparison tied to the descriptions supplied. If the current-state record contains no preparation-time measurement, do not calculate a numerical difference by inventing one. You can still identify the documented qualitative difference and preserve any missing information as a question.

The output is a gap list grounded in current and desired states. It does not itself prove the cause of every gap or choose the solution. The immediate analytical task is to make the difference explicit enough that stakeholders can see what has to change. The example’s numbers and one-place objective illustrate that comparison, not a claim about a real organization.
tb5 A gap is the difference between current and desired states, not simply a complaint or a proposed fix. “Buy a new system” does not identify the difference. Compare corresponding descriptions and state what separates the business from its goal.

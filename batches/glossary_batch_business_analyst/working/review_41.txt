The relation has no duplicate rows, and the sequence of rows and columns is insignificant to its relational meaning. If two customer rows are displayed in a different order, the display position is not itself a customer attribute. Do not use “the third row” as the business identity of a customer.

The useful mental model is a named table with meaningful fields and related values in each record, within a database of multiple tables. The sample values are hypothetical and the treatment is the relational model. The model describes the data structure, not every behavior a database product may permit. First be able to identify a table, row, and column in the business data.
